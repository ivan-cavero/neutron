## 1. Hallazgo (vanilla 26.2 CFR + javap)

`WorldgenRandom.setFeatureSeed(long seed, int index, int step)` es:

```
seed + (long)index + (long)(10000 * step)
```

Neutron tenía **index y step intercambiados**: `seed + step + 10000*index`.

Eso desvía el RNG de **todas** las features (sculk, ores, árboles).

Además `ChunkGenerator.applyBiomeDecoration` usa el índice **global** de `FeatureSorter.buildFeaturesPerStep`, no el índice local del bioma. Probe `tools/java-probe/src/ProbeFeatureOrder.java`:

| step | sculk_vein | sculk_patch | ore_dirt | ore_copper | trees_plains |
|------|----------:|------------:|---------:|-----------:|-------------:|
| 6/7/9 | 0 (step 7) | 1 (step 7) | 0 | 25 | 52 |

Step 7 overworld: `[sculk_vein, sculk_patch_deep_dark, sulfur_spike_cluster, sulfur_spike, dripstone_cluster, pointed_dripstone, ore_infested]`.

## 2. Qué se hizo

1. Corregir `FeatureRandom.set_feature_seed` al CFR.
2. Port de `FeatureSorter` sobre el orden `possibleBiomes` overworld (probe).
3. `feature_dispatch` y sculk usan índice global.
4. Ores: cobre usa índice 25 (24 = `ore_copper_large`, solo dripstone).
5. Sculk: ChargeCursor alineado a CFR; **eliminado** `spend_charge_at_visited` (heurística). `attemptPlaceSculk` exige `hasFace` real.
